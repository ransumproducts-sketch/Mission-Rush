Privacy Policy

Relic Rush (Android) · Last updated: October 7, 2026

In short: Relic Rush has no accounts and no servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob collects to show you ads.

This Privacy Policy explains how New creatives ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Relic Rush mobile game (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This includes:

  • Your best score and number of runs played
  • Your chip balance
  • Which characters you have unlocked and which one is selected
  • Your sound setting (muted or not)

This data is not sent to us or to any server we operate. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, including rewarded ads (which you can choose to watch to earn a reward) and interstitial ads (shown between game runs, never during a run). To serve and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use:

  • Your Android Advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data, such as ads shown, clicked, or completed

We do not receive your personal information from Google. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your consent and ad choices

  • Consent form. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App shows Google's consent form on launch. Ads are only requested if you give the required consent.
  • Ad personalization. On Android, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization under Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Learn more. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Permissions

The App uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback during gameplay. It also declares the Google Advertising ID permission so that ads can work as described above. The App does not request access to your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google, through AdMob, as described in Section 2.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your game progress exists only on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes it. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies, and you can manage it through your Google Account and Android ad settings.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Security

Because your progress is stored only on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

For questions or privacy requests, email us at ransumproducts@gmail.com.
